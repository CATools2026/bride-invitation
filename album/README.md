# Your pre-shoot photographs

Your three supplied photographs are included as `01.jpg`, `02.jpg` and
`03.jpg`. Their display order and accessible descriptions are in `photos.js`.
Captions are optional.

To replace photos next week, upload the new files with the same filenames.
Once GitHub Pages publishes the update, visitors will see the new photos
when they open or refresh your invitation. To add extra photos, add their
filenames to `photos.js` as well.

The photographs need to be actual files in this published `album/` folder.
